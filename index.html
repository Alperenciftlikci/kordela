Kordela: Connect the Dots Game

Connect the dots. Fill the board. Never cross.

Draw a line between each pair of dots of the same color. Fill every square on the board, and never let two lines cross. It takes seconds to learn and 500 levels to master.

How to play

Three rules. The boards do the rest.

  1. Connect

    Drag from a dot to the other dot of the same color.

  2. Fill

    Every square must be filled. A level is done when all pairs are joined and no square is empty.

  3. Never cross

    Lines can’t cross. Draw over a line and you cut it.

Beat the clock

Every level is timed. Finish faster for up to three stars. If time runs out, you get a new board of the same difficulty, so there is never one answer to memorize.

500 levels across 10 worlds

New mechanics every 20 levels

From level 21, something new joins the board every 20 levels. Each mechanic starts with three introduction levels.

  • Level 21

    Holes

    Holes stay empty. Lines go around them.

  • Level 41

    Shaped boards

    Not every board is a rectangle. Only the squares inside the shape fill.

  • Level 61

    Beads

    A bead lets only its own color through.

  • Level 81

    Bridges

    Two lines cross on a bridge, both going straight.

  • Level 101

    Straight & turn

    Go straight on stripes. Turn on knobs.

  • Level 121

    Edge portals

    Draw out through a ring and come back in at its twin.

After that, the mechanics start to mix, up to three on one board, all the way to level 500.

Play at your own pace

No ads, no sign-up and no pop-up offers. Just puzzles.

Three lamps

A lamp goes out when time runs out on a level you haven’t cleared yet. Each lamp relights after 30 minutes.

Free replays

Cleared levels stay open and never use a lamp. Go back for three stars whenever you like.

Hints when you want them

Chalk Mark shows one sure move. Dede’s Stitch draws one color’s whole line. Both cost Sequins, which you also earn by playing.

No ads, no account

There are no ads and nothing to sign up for. The game starts the moment you open it.

Made for everyone

Color-blind glyphs, Differentiate Without Color, High Contrast, Reduce Motion and a left-handed layout.

39 languages

Play in your own language, on iPhone and iPad.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-up. The game uses a random anonymous ID, never your name or email address.
  • No ads, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress is kept on your device, with an anonymous online backup.
  • Delete everything at any time from Settings in the game.
